Privacy Policy

SkillRadar Ingest · Effective 15 November 2026

HomePrivacy Policy

1. Scope

This policy covers SkillRadar Ingest (“the app”), a personal, non-commercial tool operated by its developer for private job-market research. The app is used with one dedicated Gmail mailbox belonging to the developer. It is not offered to other users.

2. Google permission requested

The app requests a single OAuth scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly. It cannot send, delete, move, label or otherwise change any email or setting. No other Google scope is requested.

3. Data the app accesses

  • The app lists and downloads only messages sent by jobalerts-noreply@linkedin.com (LinkedIn job-alert emails). Other messages are not requested.
  • From each alert it reads the email headers (sender, subject, date, message ID and authentication results, used to confirm the message really came from LinkedIn) and the body.
  • From the body it extracts public job-listing information: LinkedIn job ID, job title, company name and location.

4. How the data is used

  • Solely to build a private dataset of job listings for aggregated research on skills and requirements in the Israeli job market.
  • The data is not sold, rented, shared or transferred to any third party; it is not used for advertising, credit-worthiness or lending decisions, or to build user profiles.
  • Gmail data is not used to train generalized AI or machine-learning models, and it is not sent to any third-party AI service. Any analysis runs on the developer's own computer.
  • No human other than the developer reads the data. The developer may look at stored alert emails only to debug parsing errors.

5. Storage and security

  • All data is stored only on the developer's own computer. There is no server, cloud database or hosted backend.
  • OAuth credentials and tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain. They are never written to source code, logs or plain-text files.
  • Downloaded alert emails are stored compressed, with file permissions restricted to the developer's user account.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Raw alert emails that were processed successfully are deleted 30 days after download.
  • Raw alert emails that could not be processed are kept for up to 180 days so the error can be fixed and the email reprocessed, and are then deleted.
  • After deletion, only minimal records remain: the message ID, date, sender, subject, a SHA-256 fingerprint, the processing status, and the extracted job-listing fields from section 3. These are kept to prevent duplicates and support research statistics.
  • Emails in the Gmail mailbox itself are never deleted or changed by the app.

7. Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After revocation, the app can no longer read the mailbox, and the developer can delete all locally stored data.

8. Compliance with Google API Services User Data Policy

SkillRadar Ingest's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

9. Changes and contact

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated before the change takes effect. Questions: skillradar191@gmail.com.

SkillRadar is an independent personal project and is not affiliated with LinkedIn or Google.
