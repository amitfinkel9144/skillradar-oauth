What it does

SkillRadar is a private research project that studies which skills and requirements Israeli employers ask for in Product Manager, Product Owner, System Analyst and AI Product Manager roles. It is not a job-application tool and does not apply to jobs, contact employers or post anything.

SkillRadar Ingest is the component that signs in with Google. Once a day it reads LinkedIn job-alert emails from a single dedicated Gmail mailbox that belongs to the project owner, extracts the job identifiers listed in each alert (job title, company, location and LinkedIn job ID), and stores them on the owner's own computer for later analysis.

Google permissions

  • The app requests one permission only: gmail.readonly (read email messages and settings).
  • It cannot send, delete, label or modify any email.
  • It only fetches messages from the sender jobalerts-noreply@linkedin.com; other mail is not requested.

Who can use it

The app is used by its developer only, with one dedicated mailbox. It is not offered to the public, and there are no other users.

Read the Privacy Policy for details on what data is accessed, how it is stored, and when it is deleted.
